About Us

Our Story & Mission

ZP Model High School, Gadiwat is a premier institution committed to the holistic development of every student. Established under the Zilla Parishad, we have been a beacon of quality education for the youth of the region.

Our school blends strong academic foundations with co-curricular activities, sports, and cultural programs to help students realize their fullest potential. Our motto — एकच ध्यास विद्यार्थी विकास — reflects our singular dedication to student growth.

We strive to build responsible, knowledgeable, and compassionate citizens ready to contribute to society and the nation.
